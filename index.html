How to read it

What the bands are

  • The publisher writes one file per quarter hour, about a minute after it closes. This page re-reads the data every 15 minutes; nothing needs reloading by hand.
  • The first forecast of a day is at 10:15. Each model reads a completed sixty-minute window and no window crosses the overnight gap, so the session's first twelve 5-minute bars have to print first.
  • Hourly bands stop at 14:30 and 15-minute bands at 15:15, the last marks whose horizon still ends by the 15:30 close.
  • The band is the forecast; the median line is not. Over Feb–Jun 2026 out of sample, the 80% bands covered 80.0% (hourly) and 79.9% (15-minute), within 1.3 and 0.5 points in every volatility regime.
  • Bands are OG_CARR — a seasonal CARR range forecast placed symmetrically on the current price, then adaptively calibrated. On Feb–Jun 2026 out of sample it scored 6–15% better (Winkler/pinball) than the earlier separate high/low bands at the same 80% coverage. Direction is still a separate model.
  • Direction is a separate model and a genuinely harder call than the range; read the bars as a lean, not a certainty.

Forecasts, not advice. Nothing about costs, slippage or execution is modelled.
