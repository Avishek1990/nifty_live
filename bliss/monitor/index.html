Direction

—

Every hour from 10:15 to 15:15, the price is read against the day forecast in force: the 10:15 re-issue (the 09:15 forecast where there is none). d high = (price − H75) / (H75 − H25) and d low = (L75 − price) / (L25 − L75), with L75 the deeper low level. The flag is the calibrated call: a logistic model of d (refitted every quarter on earlier sessions only) gives P(the next hour closes up); ▲ up when it is above 50%, otherwise ▼ down. Its confidence (0–100) ranks how far P(up) is from 50% against the hours the model was trained on, and the darker the colour, the more confident the call. A filled marker went the flagged way over the next hour, a hollow one went the other way; the 15:15 reading has no full hour left in the session. Click a session to chart it.
