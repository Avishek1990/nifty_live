News calibration

The forecasts adjusted for the news

A thin layer on top of the forecasts; the forecasts themselves are unchanged. As issued is the forecast itself. Daily news moves each level by an amount learned from earlier sessions, using the daily news signal (mood, loudness, news impact on the gap and on the size of the day) of today and the two sessions before, all published by 05:30. Daily + live adds the live news from GDELT's 15-minute article files, up to 15 minutes before the mark: the share of Indian articles about market drivers (RBI, inflation, the rupee, crude, the Fed, tariffs and so on) and their tone, since the open and over the last hour. Every 15 minutes from 10:15 the day's 10:15 re-issue and the current hour's forecast are re-adjusted, never inside the high or low already made; the main forecast and its targets and stop losses follow the latest adjustment.

How the version 1 calibration scored out of sample

Walk-forward, refitted every quarter from 2021. Error: average pinball loss in % (levels) or log loss (probabilities); lower is better. Recalibrated = the same correction without the news inputs. “News − recal.” is the difference with its 95% bootstrap range over sessions; below 0 means the news version had the lower loss. These scores are for the daily-news version on the 09:15 day forecast, today-vs-yesterday and the 15-minute hour forecast of the backtest; the hourly re-adjustments and the 10:15 re-issue's adjustment have not been scored yet.
