News calibration

The forecasts adjusted for the news

A thin layer on top of the models; the models themselves are unchanged. Base is the model's own forecast. News (v1) moves each level by an amount learned from earlier sessions, using the daily news signal (mood, loudness, news impact on the gap and on the size of the day) of today and the two sessions before, all published by 05:30. v2 adds the real-time news from GDELT's 15-minute article files, up to 15 minutes before the mark: the share of Indian articles about market drivers (RBI, inflation, the rupee, crude, the Fed, tariffs and so on) and their tone, since the open and over the last hour. At each mark the day's OPEN levels are re-issued, never inside the high or low already made.
