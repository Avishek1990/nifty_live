The strategies

Backtest: the Simulate all run the shortlist was picked from, on the 09:15 forecast and the 10:15 re-issue without news (the news-calibrated versions exist only from October 2026). Forward: the sessions from the start date, each recorded once after its close. Per session = trades divided by the sessions the strategy could trade. Each pick has three twins with the same entry and exits in NIFTY points from the entry price: target 100 (out at +100 points, no stop), stop 20 (out at −20 points, no target) and target 100 · stop 20 (both); otherwise held to the exit time.
